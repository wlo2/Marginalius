# Tools for thought. Modernized.

## [Outlineus](https://github.com/wlo2/Outlineus)

Shows which sections of a note are on screen in Obsidian's Outline view, with a depth track and a moving dot. It can also keep headings collapsed between sessions and show their Markdown. 

## [Marginalius](https://github.com/wlo2/Marginalius)

Adaptive marginalia for Obsidian. Notes sit in the page margins on a wide screen and turn into small boxes in the text when space is tight.
